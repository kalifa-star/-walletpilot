Explanations are scripted, not live AI. A separate rules engine validates proposals. Pause blocks new orders; it leaves open positions exposed to price changes. Risk checks still run when you advance the replay.

Withdraw closes open positions, charges the displayed trading and conversion costs, and returns the virtual balance. Nothing connects to a real wallet, broker, or OPay account.
